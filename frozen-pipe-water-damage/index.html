A frozen pipe becomes an emergency when it thaws and starts to pour. Shut off the main water valve, open a faucet to relieve pressure, and call the 24/7 line. Keep the heat on, move valuables out of the wet room and stay away from outlets near the water.

Frozen pipe water damage is the flooding and soaking that follows a pipe that expanded with ice, split and then released water as the ice melted. The split is often a small one that opens up only when the temperature rises.

Why Frozen Pipes Still Happen in Sun City Center.

We do not claim that freezes are common here, because the numbers do not say so. For the actual count of freezing days in the Tampa Bay area, look at the climate normals published by the National Weather Service office in Tampa Bay. What we know is how pipes fail when a freeze does arrive.

Florida homes are built for heat, not cold. Supply lines run through attics, along exterior walls, in unheated garages and outside to irrigation lines and backflow devices. None of that is insulated the way it would be farther north, so even a short hard freeze can split a line that has never given trouble before.

What to Do When a Frozen Pipe Thaws.

  • Shut off the main water valve before you do anything else.
  • Open the lowest faucet so remaining water can drain from the lines.
  • Keep indoor heat on and do not use open flames to thaw a pipe.
  • Photograph the damage before moving wet furniture.
  • Call the 24/7 line and describe where the pipe runs.

After hours is when most freeze damage gets reported, because the pipes thaw in the morning and the water runs while people are at work or asleep. The dispatch line stays open 24/7, and a same-day response is available when a crew is free.
