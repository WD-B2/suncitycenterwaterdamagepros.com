Getting Help to a Rural Address.

Give the dispatcher your street address and any landmarks, such as the nearest crossroad or a gate code. Rural driveways can be hard to find at night, and a clear description saves time.

If your power is out, a charged phone and a flashlight help. Keep the call short, describe the water and let the crew plan equipment before they arrive.

Wells and pumps add one more thing to check. If a pressure tank or pump line failed, shut off the breaker before you step into wet ground. Dry the pump house after the crew pumps it out, since motors and wiring hate moisture.

For roof problems on barns and sheds, see ceiling water damage repair, which also covers roof leaks. A roofer closes the opening and we dry what got wet.
