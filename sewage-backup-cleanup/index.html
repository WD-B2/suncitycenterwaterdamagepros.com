Drains Gurgling, Toilet Overflowing or Sewage in the Tub? That Is a Sewage Backup.

If water comes up through a floor drain, a tub or a toilet and it smells like sewage, the line is blocked or overwhelmed. The material that comes back is Category 3 water, which is what the cleanup industry calls water that carries pathogens.

Sewage is an emergency. Stop using sinks, showers, toilets and washers, keep children and pets out, and call the 24/7 line. Do not walk through it barefoot, and do not try to clean it with towels. If you are on a county sewer, the utility may need to clear the main.

Sewage backup cleanup is the removal of contaminated water and the porous materials it touched, followed by cleaning, disinfecting and drying. Hard surfaces can be cleaned, but carpet, pad and soaked drywall are usually removed.

Why a Sewage Backup Is an Emergency.

Sewage carries pathogens and spreads fast through porous materials, so every hour matters. It is an emergency the moment it reaches a floor. Keep people out, stop using drains and call the line at any hour.

What Safe Sewage Cleanup Looks Like.

  1. Contain and protect.

    The crew wears gloves, boots and respirators and keeps the contamination from spreading to clean rooms.

    .

  2. Extract and remove.

    Standing sewage is pumped out. Carpet, pad, insulation and soaked drywall come out and are bagged.

    .

  3. Clean and disinfect.

    Hard surfaces are washed and treated with an antimicrobial, then rinsed.

    .

  4. Dry and verify.

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure, and readings confirm the space is ready for rebuilding.

    .

This work follows the practices described in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it. See water extraction and structural drying for the first and last steps.

Crews working in Apollo Beach have seen how storm flooding can overwhelm sewer systems.

After the Backup: Odors, Air and Rebuilding.

Once the contaminated material is out, the smell should fade as the space dries and is disinfected. A lingering odor can mean sewage soaked into a subfloor or wall cavity, which the crew checks with moisture meters before anything is rebuilt.

Keep the affected rooms closed off until the crew says they are ready. Rebuilding comes last, after readings confirm the structure is dry and the surfaces are clean. If the backup left visible mold, read mold remediation for the next step.

What to Do Before Protective-Gear Crews Arrive.

  • Stop using sinks, tubs, toilets and washers.
  • Keep children and pets out of the affected area.
  • Do not walk through the water barefoot.
  • Do not run a household vacuum on sewage.
  • Wash hands well after any contact.

What affects the cost of sewage cleanup.
