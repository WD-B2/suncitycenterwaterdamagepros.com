Air Conditioners, Water Heaters and Roofs.

Florida air conditioners produce condensate in humid weather, and a clogged drain line can back water into a closet or ceiling. Turn the system off at the thermostat when that happens and call. A water heater that leaks needs its supply closed, and a roof leak needs photos and a roofer for the opening.

We handle the wet materials after the source is stopped. A plumber, an air conditioning contractor or a roofer fixes the cause. Professionals commonly refer to the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, which describes how to classify water and plan drying, and reading it never replaces a moisture assessment at your home.

Safe Steps Before a Crew Arrives.

Close the supply.

Shut the valve at the fixture or the main when you can reach it dry.

Keep off wet floors near power.

If water touches outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility.

Lift valuables.

Move rugs, books and electronics up and away from the water.

Record it.

Photograph each wet room and note when you found the water.

Drying a villa follows the same steps as any home. Pumps and extractors remove water, then air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure to readings. Kings Point, a separate gated community across town, has its own condominium-focused page.

Lanais, Tile Floors and Cabinets.

Many homes here have tile floors and screened lanais. Tile itself does not soak up water, but grout lines, the subfloor and the toe kicks under cabinets can hold moisture long after the surface looks dry. Moisture readings along the cabinet base and the bottom of the wall tell you more than your eyes do.

If water crossed a lanai threshold, tell the dispatcher, because a door track or a sliding glass door frame can carry water into the room. Cabinets and baseboards that stay wet are the usual places where a small leak turns into a larger repair.

Documents to Gather.

Keep the policy declarations page, any association notice and the photos you took. Single-family owners look at a homeowners policy, while villa and condominium owners also read their association documents.
