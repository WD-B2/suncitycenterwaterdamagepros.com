Musty Smell, Dark Spots or a Stain That Returns? Moisture Is the Real Problem.

If a room smells musty, a ceiling stain keeps coming back, or dark spots appear on a wall or in a corner, there is a moisture source feeding growth. Mold needs water to live, so finding and drying the source comes first. Removal without that step only buys time.

Mold remediation is the controlled removal or cleaning of mold-affected materials, followed by cleaning, drying and a check that the work is complete. It is different from a quick wipe with a household cleaner.

In Florida, the state licenses mold assessors and mold remediators under Chapter 468, Part XVI of the Florida Statutes, through the Department of Business and Professional Regulation. The rules also separate assessment from remediation on the same project, with a 12-month limit on one firm doing both.

How a Florida Mold Job Is Organized.

  1. Assess.

    A licensed assessor inspects the property, finds the extent of growth and writes a protocol for the work.

    .

  2. Fix the moisture.

    The water source is repaired and wet materials are dried to readings.

    .

  3. Contain and remove.

    The area is sealed, air is filtered and affected materials are removed or cleaned following the protocol.

    .

  4. Clear.

    A post-remediation check, often with sampling, confirms the work met the protocol.

    .

The ANSI/IICRC S520 standard describes mold remediation practices. You can ask any company whether its crews follow it. We do not make any promise about how a surface will look or behave in the future.

Test results are most useful when a licensed assessor interprets them. A number on a lab sheet means little without the context of the building and the outdoor air.

Mold Clearance Testing for a Home Sale or Loan.

If you are selling or refinancing, a buyer or lender may ask for proof that a mold problem was resolved. A clearance report from an independent licensed assessor gives that record. Keep the protocol, the invoices and the clearance report together.

Planning ahead helps. Fix the moisture, document the work and ask the assessor what the buyer or lender will accept. Requirements differ by transaction.

What to Do Before Anyone Starts Mold Work.

  • Stop the water source.
  • Keep the room closed and the air handler off.
  • Do not sand, scrub or spray the growth.
  • Take dated photos for the assessor and your insurer.
  • Ask to see the state license of any assessor or remediator and confirm it with the state.

What affects the cost of mold remediation.

Cost follows the area affected, the materials involved, containment needs and the testing steps. A small contained area costs less than growth through several rooms.
