A burst pipe is an emergency the moment you hear it. Shut off the main water valve first, then call the 24/7 line. Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely, and move anything valuable away from the spray.

Burst pipe water damage is the flooding, soaking and structural wetting that follows a supply line failure. In Sun City Center it usually starts inside a wall, an attic or a utility closet, so the water has often been running for a while before anyone finds it.

When a Burst Pipe Becomes an Emergency.

A pipe that splits under pressure can release many gallons in minutes. It is an emergency when water is still flowing, when it reaches outlets or ceilings, or when you cannot find the shutoff. Close the main, open a low faucet and call.

First Ten Minutes After a Pipe Bursts.

  1. Find the main valve.

    The house shutoff is usually near where the line enters the home or at the meter. Close it fully, then open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the lines.

    .

  2. Protect the electrical system.

    If water is near outlets, a panel or a ceiling light fixture, stay out of the room and call the electric utility or 911 first.

    .

  3. Document the damage.

    Take photos and video before you move anything. A dated record helps any insurance claim.

    .

  4. Call the 24/7 line.

    Tell us where the pipe is, what material it is made of, and which rooms are wet. Same-day response is available when a crew is free.

    .

Where Burst Pipes Hide in a Slab-Era House.

Many homes in this community were built decades ago, and the plumbing is part of that age. Copper, CPVC and PEX lines each fail in their own way, through pinholes, brittle fittings, loose connections or a pressure spike. Water often shows up first as a soft spot in the carpet, a stain on the ceiling below an attic run, or a drywall bulge near a shared wall in a duplex.

Hillsborough County Water Resources supplies the water, and a leak on the county side of the meter is a different call than one inside the house. If you are unsure which side the break is on, shut off the main valve at the house and ask the utility.

Drying and Repair After the Pipe Is Fixed.

A plumber fixes the pipe, and we handle the water. The crew extracts standing water, opens the wet wall where needed and runs air movers and dehumidifiers while taking daily moisture readings. The process is covered in structural drying and dehumidification.

When a burst runs through the kitchen or hall cabinetry, kitchen water damage restoration covers cabinets and counters. Crews working near Apollo Beach and Wimauma use the same drying standards.
