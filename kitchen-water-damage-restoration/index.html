A kitchen has more water sources than any other room: the sink, dishwasher, refrigerator ice line and disposal. A slow leak often runs for weeks inside a cabinet before the floor shows it. If you see warping, swelling or a musty smell, call and we will check with meters.

Where Kitchen Water Hides.

Spot.Common cause.What we check.
Under the sink.Drain or supply connection.Cabinet floor, back panel, subfloor.
Behind the dishwasher.Hose, pump or door seal.Floor edge, toe kick, adjoining cabinet.
Behind the refrigerator.Ice maker line.Floor under the fridge, wall base.
Around the range hood or wall.Pipe in the wall.Drywall, backsplash edge.

Kitchen cabinets are often particleboard with a laminate face. Particleboard swells when it gets wet and does not usually return to shape. Solid wood and plywood dry better. Part of the work is deciding what to dry and what to replace.

Countertops, stone and tile are usually safe. The cabinet boxes and the toe kick are what suffer, along with vinyl or laminate flooring that trapped water below.

Drying a Kitchen Without Tearing It Out.

The crew pulls the toe kick, drills small holes if needed and uses air movers and dehumidifiers to dry cabinet bases. A wet floor is dried through flooring where possible. If particleboard has swollen, we say so and list options.

You get readings, not guesses. See structural drying for how we confirm dryness, and appliance leak water damage for dishwasher and fridge sources.

Living in a Kitchen Under Repair.

Drying equipment runs for several days, so plan for meals elsewhere. Keep food away from the work area and turn off the dishwasher. Your Sun City Center neighbors in townhomes and duplexes may share a wall, so tell your neighbor if water could have crossed.

Signs of a Slow Kitchen Leak.

  • A musty smell when you open the cabinet under the sink.
  • Cabinet floors that feel soft or look swollen at the edges.
  • Flooring that lifts or gaps near the dishwasher.
  • A water bill that climbs with no change in use.
  • Stains on the ceiling in the room below, if there is one.

A slow leak is easier to dry than a burst line but harder to find, because the water hides. Pull everything out of the cabinet, dry the floor of it with a towel and watch for new drips for a day. If it stays damp, call for a meter check.

Small leaks that go unnoticed can feed mold in dark, closed spaces. See mold remediation if you see growth. Do not paint over stains. The surface looks fixed while the wood stays wet.

We work with your plumber, not instead of one. The plumber fixes the source and we dry the damage. Neighbors in Balm and Sun City Center describe the same pattern with older appliances.

What to Do Before You Touch a Wet Kitchen.

  • Close the supply valve to the sink, dishwasher or refrigerator line.
  • Stay away from outlets and appliances that are wet.
  • Empty the cabinet under the leak.
  • Do not run the dishwasher.
  • Photograph the cabinets, the floor and the source.

What affects the cost of kitchen water damage.

Cost follows the number of cabinets, the flooring type and the days of drying. Cabinets that must be replaced are the largest variable.
