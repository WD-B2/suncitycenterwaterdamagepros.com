A leaking water heater is an emergency. Turn off the electric breaker or the gas valve to the unit, close the cold water supply line to the tank, and call the 24/7 line. Do not touch the heater while you stand in water.

Water heater leak cleanup is the removal of the water a failed tank or fitting released and the drying of the floors and walls around it. The tank itself is a plumber's job, so we handle the water and the damage.

When a Leaking Tank Turns Into an Emergency.

A slow drip from a fitting is a repair visit. A rusted tank seam that sprays or runs is an emergency, because a standard tank holds 40 to 50 gallons and the supply keeps refilling it until the valve is closed. After hours, the line is staffed and same-day response is available when a crew is free.

Look for water pooled under the tank, rust streaks on the base, a puddle that returns after you wipe it and a rumbling noise from the tank. Any of them is worth a call before the water reaches the baseboards.

Where the Water Goes From a Closet or Garage Heater.

Heaters sit in garages, hall closets and utility rooms. In a slab-on-grade house the water runs out the door of the closet, under the wall and into the carpet or tile in the next room. Garage water often crosses the threshold into the house.

Pans and drain lines are meant to catch small leaks, but they cannot handle a seam failure. The temperature and pressure relief valve can also discharge when it is stuck or when the tank is overheating, which is something a plumber should check.

Drying the Closet, the Floor and the Next Room.

The crew extracts standing water, then checks the wall base and flooring with moisture meters. Baseboards and the bottom of drywall are often cut out so air can reach the wet cavity, and air movers dry the space with a dehumidifier. See water extraction and structural drying for the process.

If the heater sits next to a kitchen or laundry, water often tracks to cabinets, which kitchen water damage restoration covers.

Crews serving Wimauma and Sun City Center handle many of these calls on slab homes, where hot water lines also run under the floor.

What affects the cost of a water heater leak cleanup.

A leak caught in minutes may need only extraction and a day of drying. One that ran overnight can reach flooring, drywall and cabinetry.
