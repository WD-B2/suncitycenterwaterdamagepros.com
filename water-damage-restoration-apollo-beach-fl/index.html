Hurricane Season Along the Bay.

Apollo Beach sits near Tampa Bay, so storm surge and heavy rain are the main risks. Hillsborough County reported in September 2024 that flooding from Hurricane Helene in Apollo Beach and Ruskin damaged wastewater pumping systems.

The county also has a HEAT tool for evacuation zones, which run from A to E. Evacuation zones are not flood zones, so use the Find My Flood Zone tool for your address.

Summer Rain and Drainage.

The National Weather Service in Tampa Bay reports that most local rain falls from June through September. Canals and drains such as the San Juan Canal can fill quickly in a heavy rain.

Water that comes in from outside should be treated as contaminated. Read sewage backup cleanup and storm water damage cleanup for safe steps.

After the Water Goes Down.

Once the water recedes, the work shifts to removal and drying. Wet drywall, insulation and carpet that touched surge water come out, and the structure is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers until readings match a dry reference.

Keep every receipt and photo. Florida Statutes section 627.70132 sets deadlines for property insurance notice, and the Department of Financial Services helpline can answer process questions.

Drying near the bay takes patience because the outdoor air is humid. See structural drying for how crews keep the equipment working on the room and not the weather.

Mold can start in wet materials left in place, so quick removal matters. Read mold remediation if you notice a musty smell weeks later.
