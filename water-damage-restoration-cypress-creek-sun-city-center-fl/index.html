What Is Inside Cypress Creek.

Directories and builder announcements name several sections. Cypress Mill and The Maples at Cypress Creek, a townhouse community, are listed as Lennar builds from about 2019 into 2023. La Paloma Village and Cypress Creek Village are older, with directory build years reaching back to 1988 and 1989. Mattamy Homes announced The Preserve at La Paloma as a gated 55 plus section of 104 attached villas and single-family homes, part of the Villages of Cypress Creek.

Because ages and rules differ from section to section, we ask what you live in rather than assume. A home does not leak because of its build year, and a moisture meter tells us more than a directory does.

When Townhouses Share a Wall.

Attached homes can pass moisture through shared walls and floor framing. Tell us which side of the wall the water came from, whether the neighbor is home and whether the building has a shared roof. Drying the wet side but not the other can leave moisture behind.

Photograph the wall, the floor and any stains on the ceiling below. Those photos help you, your association and an insurer see where the water went.

Drain and Sewer Backups.

Listings in the area show public sewer, and a backup is its own kind of emergency. Stop using sinks, tubs and toilets, keep people and pets out and do not try to dry it with household fans or a shop vacuum. Contaminated water needs protective gear and a different plan from clean water, a distinction the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard describes as water categories.

Read sewage backup cleanup to see how those jobs work. If water is rising from a drain, call the utility as well as us.

Heavy Rain and What Was Not Verified.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status was not verified for Cypress Creek. Mapped zones apply property by property, so use the county Find My Flood Zone tool for your address, and keep in mind that Zone X does not rule out flooding. Hurricane flood warnings in 2024 named rivers across Hillsborough County, and none of the sources reviewed for this page describes flooding in the Villages of Cypress Creek.

The National Weather Service says most of the Tampa Bay area rain falls from June through September. If rain finds a roof or window, ceiling water damage repair explains what to do first.

Safe Steps Before a Crew Arrives.

Shut the source.

Close the fixture supply or the main valve when you can reach it without stepping in water.

Avoid electrical hazards.

Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords and the panel.

Move what you can.

Lift small items and papers off wet floors if the room is safe.

Document it.

Photograph the damage and write the time down for your insurer or association.

What to Tell the Dispatcher.

  • Your street address and the section name, such as Cypress Mill or La Paloma.
  • Whether the home is detached, a townhouse or an attached villa.
  • Which wall, ceiling or floor is wet, and whether a neighbor shares it.
  • The gate code or a contact who can open the gate.

That short list lets the right equipment go on the truck and avoids a wasted trip to the wrong gate.

Paperwork Worth Keeping.

Keep your policy declarations page, any association notice and the photos and moisture readings from the job. Coverage depends on the cause of the damage and the policy terms, so ask your insurer and association what applies. We cannot decide coverage for you.
