Basement water damage cleanup is the pumping, removal of wet materials and drying of a below-grade or lower-level space after water enters from rain, groundwater, drains or plumbing.

When Lower-Level Water Is an Emergency.

Water in a lower room is an emergency when it is rising, when it touches the panel or a furnace, or when you smell sewage. Keep out of standing water near power and call the line.

Do Homes Here Even Have Basements?

Basements are rare in the Sun City Center area because the water table is high and most homes are built on slabs. What you may have is a sunken room, a lower garage, a utility area or a basement in a house that came from another region. We handle all of them.

We will not cite a local frequency, since no primary source exists for it. The mechanism is simple: a space lower than its surroundings collects water, and Florida's rainy season from June through September, which the National Weather Service in Tampa Bay says brings most of the area's rain, tests that low point.

Pumping, Removing and Drying.

The crew pumps standing water, then removes wet carpet, pad, insulation and drywall that cannot be dried. Concrete and block walls hold water, so dehumidifiers run for days. Readings tell us when it is ready for repair.

If the water came from outside or a sewer line, treat it as contaminated and read sewage backup cleanup. Storm sources are covered in storm water damage cleanup.

Restoration Is Not Waterproofing.

We restore water damage. We do not seal walls, install sump systems or fix drainage. If the same space keeps flooding, a contractor must find the cause. We can tell you what the readings show so that contractor knows where to look.

Ask us for the moisture map when you call a drainage or foundation contractor.

Before the Crew Arrives.

  • Do not enter standing water if power is on in the space.
  • Move valuables up and out if you can do it from dry ground.
  • Note the water line on the walls and take photos.
  • Shut off the water to any appliance in the space if the supply is the source.

Tell us what is stored below, such as a water heater, a furnace or a freezer. Mechanical equipment changes how we plan the pumping. Do not run a pump on an extension cord through water.

Wet storage boxes and papers should be moved to a dry room and spread out, and wet carpet is covered in carpet water damage restoration. Cardboard collapses fast, and paper can mold in a few days. Our 24/7 line is open at any hour, and crews arrive same-day when one is free.

Hillsborough County has a Find My Flood Zone tool that shows if an address sits in a mapped flood area. Keep in mind that evacuation zones are not the same thing as flood zones.

What affects the cost of lower-level water cleanup.

Cost follows the water depth, the area, the finished materials and the days of drying. An unfinished utility room costs less than a finished lower level.
