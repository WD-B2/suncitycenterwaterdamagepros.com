Burst Pipes and Supply Line Leaks.

A pipe that bursts in a wall or under a sink can release a lot of water fast. Shut off the main, open a faucet low in the house to relieve pressure and call. See burst pipe water damage for what happens next.

Older homes in town may have older supply lines, and a connection that has been weeping for years can fail suddenly. A plumber fixes the line and we handle the water.

Storm Water and River Flooding.

The Little Manatee River gauge near Wimauma, at US 301, has a flood stage of 11 feet. Hillsborough County issued flood warnings for the river during Hurricane Debby in August 2024 and again during Hurricane Milton in October 2024.

When storm water enters a home, treat it as contaminated until a crew says otherwise. Read flood damage restoration for the process, and use the county Find My Flood Zone tool to check your address.

Newer Neighborhoods and Older Homes.

Wimauma has both older houses and newer communities, such as Valencia Lakes. Newer homes tend to have slab foundations and enclosed attics, which affects where water travels. Slab homes can have hidden leaks, covered on slab leak water damage.

Fire Station 22 on 7th Street serves the area, and the county water utility handles public water. Hillsborough County Water Resources can be reached to report a main line problem.

What to Do Before We Arrive in Wimauma.
