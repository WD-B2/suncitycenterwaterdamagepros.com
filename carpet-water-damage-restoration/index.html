Carpet Water Damage in Sun City Center, FL.

Carpet and pad act like a sponge. Quick extraction and drying can save carpet from clean water, and we tell you when it cannot be saved.

Wet carpet starts to smell in a day or two and can grow mold in a few more. Pull furniture off, blot what you can and call. Clean-water carpet that is extracted fast is often saved. Carpet soaked by sewage or flood water usually is not.

Save It or Replace It: How the Decision Is Made.

Water type.Time wet.Usual outcome.
Clean (supply line, rain through roof).Under 48 hours.Often dried in place.
Clean.Several days.Pad replaced, carpet maybe saved.
Gray (dishwasher, washer).Any.Pad replaced, carpet case by case.
Black (sewage, flood).Any.Carpet and pad removed.

The pad under carpet holds water longest and is the cheapest part to replace. Many jobs lift the carpet, discard the pad, dry the subfloor and reinstall the carpet over new pad.

We use moisture meters to check the pad, the tack strip and the slab or subfloor below. Lifting a corner shows more than looking at the surface.

How Carpet Is Dried.

  1. Extract.

    A truck-mounted or portable extractor pulls water from the carpet and pad.

    .

  2. Lift and float.

    The carpet is lifted at the edge and floated on air movers so air passes beneath.

    .

  3. Dry.

    Dehumidifiers remove moisture from the room while air movers work the carpet and subfloor.

    .

  4. Verify.

    Meter readings confirm carpet, pad and subfloor are dry before the carpet goes back down.

    .

Most of the homes in Sun City Center have carpet over concrete slabs. Concrete holds water, so the slab must dry before the carpet is stretched back. See structural drying for how we confirm it.

If the leak came from a pipe in the floor, read slab leak water damage. For flooded rooms, flood damage restoration covers contaminated water.

Odor, Stains and Mold on Carpet.

A musty smell means moisture is still present. Deodorizing without drying only masks it. If you see growth on the back of the carpet or on tack strips, stop and read mold remediation.

Stains from tannins in wood or dye from furniture may stay after drying. We tell you before work starts if a stain is unlikely to come out. Our 24/7 line is open, and a same-day response is available when a crew is free.

Furniture, Tack Strips and Everything Under the Carpet.

Furniture legs leave rust and tannin stains on wet carpet, so lift pieces onto foil or plastic blocks as soon as you can. Move rugs and boxes to a dry room, and keep electronics off the floor.

Tack strips are wood and can stay wet after the carpet looks dry. The crew checks them and the baseboards, because wet trim keeps moisture in the wall base. A thorough job looks at the whole edge of the room.

Questions to Ask Before You Agree to a Plan.

  • Will the pad be replaced, and why?
  • What are the moisture readings of the subfloor today and what is the target?
  • How many air movers and dehumidifiers will run, and for how many days?
  • What happens if the carpet cannot be saved?
  • What is the written scope and the price before work starts?

A clear answer to each of these tells you the company has a plan. Vague answers about "treating" the carpet are a warning sign. We answer all five before work begins.

What affects the cost of carpet water damage.

Cost follows square footage, whether the pad is replaced, the water type and the drying days. Saving carpet costs less than replacing it, when it works.
