Water Damage Service Area Around Sun City Center, FL.

We are based in Sun City Center and serve nearby towns in southern Hillsborough County.

Call (813) 738-0233

Our office is at 1601 Sun City Center Plaza, Sun City Center, FL 33573. Crews drive from there to the communities below. The 24/7 line answers at any hour, and a same-day response is available when a crew is free.

Sun City Center is an unincorporated community in Hillsborough County. Its 2020 Census population was 30,952. It sits near I-75 at Exit 240, with US 301 to the east and the Little Manatee River to the south.

Towns and What Sets Each One Apart.

Wimauma.

About six miles east on SR 674. Near the Little Manatee River gauge at US 301, with river flooding as a seasonal concern.

Balm.

Rural and agricultural, about eight miles east. Wells, septic systems and outbuildings shape the work.

Apollo Beach.

About nine miles north near Tampa Bay. Storm surge and canal drainage are the main risks.

Neighborhoods Inside Sun City Center.

Kings Point.

Gated condominium villas south of State Road 674, where associations and shared walls shape the first call.

Renaissance.

Golf community of single-family homes, villas and condominiums, including gated Verona.

Cypress Creek.

Newer townhomes, villas and homes near Interstate 75, where one leak can reach a neighbor.

Housing Types Across the Area.

Sun City Center is an age-restricted community with about 18,111 housing units, including single-family homes, duplexes, townhouses and apartments. Communities such as Kings Point, Freedom Plaza and Ashton Gardens each have their own association rules.

Wimauma has older homes and newer communities like Valencia Lakes. Balm is mostly rural, with wells, septic systems and outbuildings. Apollo Beach has waterfront and inland neighborhoods with different storm risks. The plan changes with the house.

Ruskin and Riverview.

Ruskin and Riverview sit close to Apollo Beach and Sun City Center. We do not have separate pages for them, but the same 24/7 line covers many addresses there. Call and give us the street address.

USPS lists Ruskin as an alternate name for ZIP 33572, which is why some Apollo Beach addresses show Ruskin.

The Little Manatee River gauge near Wimauma has a flood stage of 11 feet, and county flood warnings were issued there during Hurricanes Debby and Milton in 2024. Check the county Find My Flood Zone tool for your address.

If your street is not named on this page, that does not mean we cannot help. Sun City Center sits where several roads meet, and crews know the shortcuts between neighborhoods, so a call with a street address settles the question in seconds.

Tell us the nearest cross street and any gate or access code, and we plan the route before the crew leaves the office.

What We Need to Know When You Call.

  • The street address and ZIP code.
  • Whether the home sits on a slab or has a crawl space.
  • The water source, if you know it.
  • Which rooms are wet and how deep the water is.
  • Whether the water may be contaminated.

These answers let us send the right crew and equipment. A burst pipe in a slab home needs a different plan from storm water in a barn or a sewer backup in a townhouse.

Why Distance Matters Less Than You Think.

Water damage is a race against time, and the first step is often what you do before the crew arrives. Shut off the water, move valuables and stay out of rooms with water near power.

Read water extraction for what the first visit looks like and storm water damage cleanup for how storms change the plan.

What to Do Before You Call From Any Town.

  • Shut off the water at the main valve.
  • Stay out of rooms where water touches outlets.
  • Note your street address and nearest cross street.
  • Take photos of the water and the source.
  • Have your insurer's name handy if you want to mention it.
