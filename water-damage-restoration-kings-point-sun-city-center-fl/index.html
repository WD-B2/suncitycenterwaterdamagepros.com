Kings Point is the large gated community on the south side of State Road 674 in Sun City Center, with a second gate on 36th Street Southeast. The county counts it as part of Sun City Center, yet the Sun City Center Observer reports that it is separately owned and managed, with its own clubhouses and clubs.

Real estate directories put it at more than 5,000 homes and disagree on the exact count. We describe the area approximately, because no official boundary map was reviewed for this page.

Condominium Associations and Who to Call First.

Kings Point is made up largely of condominium villas, and one directory counts more than 100 separate condominium associations inside it. Minto Communities, which built later neighborhoods here, names Somerset, Nottingham, Oakley Green, Richmond and Maplewood among them.

Each association has its own governing documents, its own management contact and its own repair rules.

When water appears, two calls matter in the first hour. Your management office can close a building supply or riser you cannot reach, tell you whether a neighbor is involved and explain what notice the association wants before outside contractors work on the property. A restoration line can start planning extraction and drying. Some associations may ask for advance notice or access arrangements, so ask early rather than after equipment arrives.

These are common patterns, not rules. Your declaration of condominium and your insurance policies set the real dividing line, so read them or ask the association.

Water From the Unit Above or Next Door.

Attached villas share walls and sometimes ceilings, so water rarely stays in one place. A failed supply line in one unit can wet the drywall next door, and drying may need access to more than one home. Tell us which walls are shared, whether the neighbor knows about the leak and who holds a key if the unit next door is empty.

Read structural drying for how wet walls and floors are dried to readings, and appliance leak water damage for washer and dishwasher leaks.

Safe Steps Before a Crew Arrives in a Villa.

Stop the water.

Close the valve for the fixture or your unit. If you cannot find it, call management, because valve locations differ between buildings.

Stay clear of power.

Do not stand in water near outlets, appliances or the panel. If water is near the panel, stay out and call the electric utility.

Protect belongings.

Lift small furniture, papers and electronics onto dry surfaces when the room is safe to enter.

Photograph and note.

Take photos of the wet areas and write down the time. Share them with your association and your insurer.

Extraction and Drying in a Condominium.

Extraction comes first. Pumps and extractors pull standing water and soaked carpet pad out before it wicks into drywall and baseboards. Drying follows, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed by moisture readings that are repeated until the materials reach a dry standard.

Drying equipment is loud and draws power, which matters in a shared building. We explain what will run, where and for how long before it is set, and you can pass that on to neighbors and management. Water extraction describes the first visit step by step.

Insurance Documentation in a Condominium.

Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope can help document damage and the work done. In a condominium, insurance often has two layers, an association policy for the building and an owner policy for interior finishes and belongings, but the split depends on your documents and policy language.

Ask your association and your insurer which policy applies before repairs start. Coverage depends on the cause of the damage and the policy terms, and we cannot decide it for you.

Roofs, Heavy Rain and What Was Not Verified.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status was not verified for Kings Point. Mapped flood zones apply property by property, and Zone X does not mean flooding is impossible, so use the county Find My Flood Zone tool for your address. County flood warnings during the 2024 hurricanes named rivers such as the Little Manatee, and none of the sources reviewed for this page describes flooding inside Kings Point.

Heavy rain can still find a roof or a window seal. See ceiling water damage repair for leaks that arrive from above.
