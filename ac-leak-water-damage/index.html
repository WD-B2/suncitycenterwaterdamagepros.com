An air conditioner that is leaking water should be switched off at the thermostat, because a running system keeps making condensate. Then call a technician for the cooling system and call us for the wet ceiling, closet or floor. Our line is open 24/7.

AC leak water damage is the staining and softening of drywall, insulation and flooring after a condensate drain line or pan overflows. In Florida the system pulls gallons of water from humid air each day, so a clogged line can flood quickly.

Why Florida Air Conditioners Leak.

Cooling air removes moisture, and that moisture leaves through a drain line. Algae and sludge clog that line over time, the pan fills and water spills into the closet, the attic or the ceiling below. A frozen evaporator coil can melt into the pan and overwhelm it.

Air handlers often sit in attics or hall closets, so the leak goes unnoticed until a stain appears on a ceiling or a wall corner. Because the water is condensate, it is mostly clean at first, but it carries dust and can feed mold if it sits.

After the Drain Is Cleared: Drying the House.

The crew uses moisture meters and a thermal camera to follow the water from the unit. Wet insulation and soft drywall are removed, and air movers and a dehumidifier dry the rest. Our page on structural drying explains how dryness is confirmed.

If a ceiling stained or sagged, ceiling water damage repair covers the patching. If the stain has darkened or grown, mold remediation explains what assessment involves.

Preventing a Repeat.

Condensate lines should be flushed on a schedule that your cooling technician recommends. A float switch can shut the system off before the pan overflows. These are small additions with a big effect, and they are installed by an HVAC technician, not by us.

Hidden Wet Spots Around Ducts and Closets.

Water from an AC does not always run where you expect. It follows the duct run, soaks the insulation wrap and drips from a vent boot far from the unit. A stain in a bedroom ceiling can come from a pan in a hall closet twenty feet away.

That is why a thermal camera matters here. It shows cool, wet insulation and damp drywall that look normal on the surface, and it keeps the repair limited to the area that is really wet.

What to Do Before the Crew Arrives at an AC Leak.

  • Set the thermostat to off and switch off the air handler at its breaker.
  • Do not touch wiring near wet insulation or a flooded pan.
  • Place a container under any active drip.
  • Move furniture and rugs away from the wet area.
  • Photograph the pan, the drain line and the wet ceiling or floor.

What affects the cost of AC leak damage.

The cost depends on how long the line leaked and what the water reached. A ceiling patch is a small job and a wet attic floor with insulation is a larger one.
